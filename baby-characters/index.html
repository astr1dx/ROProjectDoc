    👶 Baby Characters¶

    Updated: November 13, 2026.

    Become a smaller version of your current class without adoption. Sister Elara offers independent baby conversion for 5,000,000z.

    Sister Elara's current location

    Sister Elara is temporarily in Prontera at 136,196, near the other service NPCs.

    /navi prontera 136/196

    Who can convert?¶

    Eligible class Baby result
    Novice Baby Novice, with a permanent job-change lock
    Swordsman, Mage, Archer, Acolyte, Merchant or Thief The corresponding baby first class
    Ninja, Taekwon or Gunslinger The corresponding baby class
    Super Novice Super Baby

    The service is for regular, non-transcended characters. Second classes and later jobs cannot convert, including Soul Linker and Star Gladiator. High Novices, transcended first classes, existing babies, already-adopted characters and characters with a pending adoption request are also refused.

    Convert with Sister Elara¶

    1. Speak to Sister Elara while on an eligible class.
    2. Read the conversion rules and confirm the 5,000,000z price.
    3. A Novice must also confirm the permanent Baby Novice restriction.
    4. The server checks eligibility and carried Zeny again before converting.

    You can cancel either confirmation without paying. A successful conversion charges the fee once. You do not need a couple, marriage or adoptive parents, and the service does not assign family relationships or grant parent-calling skills.

    What stays with your character?¶

    Your Base Level, Job Level, Base/Job EXP, learned skills, allocated stats and unspent skill points are preserved when you become the baby version of your current class.

    Normal baby-class traits and equipment restrictions apply. Equipment that the baby class cannot use is unequipped normally. The maximum base stat is 99, matching Vestige Rift's existing baby-stat adjustment.

    Job progression¶

    • Baby first classes can advance through their normal job quests and remain babies.
    • Baby Taekwon can use the Star Gladiator and Soul Linker job quests and become the corresponding baby second class.
    • Normal quest and Job Level requirements still apply. Becoming a baby does not complete a job quest for you.
    • Baby characters cannot transcend or become adults through job changes. Available progression follows the server's Episode 14.2 content limits.

    Choose your class before becoming a Baby Novice

    A Baby Novice can never change class, including to a first class or Super Baby. If you want a baby first class, choose that first class before visiting Sister Elara. To become Super Baby, become a regular Super Novice first.

    The baby class and restrictions persist after logging out and back in. Baby Novice restrictions also apply through alternative job-change routes.

    See Class Changes for class skills and Server Information for level caps and rates.
