# November 11, 2026

## Homunculus and pet commands

The four existing companion commands are now available to ordinary players, including new accounts:

- **`@hominfo`**: current homunculus HP/SP, combat stats, hunger and intimacy.
- **`@homstats`**: homunculus growth stats and possible ranges.
- **`@homtalk <message>`**: speak through an active homunculus.
- **`@pettalk <message>`**: speak or use supported emotes through an active pet.

Companion speech and supported pet emotes are logged under the owning character and account. Player chat mutes and normal configured chat-delay checks still apply.

**@settings > Mute Pets / Hide Pets** now also filter pet speech and pet emotes for each viewer. Homunculus speech and player chat remain unaffected.
