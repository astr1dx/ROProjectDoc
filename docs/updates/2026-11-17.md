# November 17, 2026

## Bio Labs 4

[Bio Labs 4](../biolabs4.md) is now open as an adapted Classic Pre-Renewal party dungeon.

- Retuned all 21 regular, elite and MVP monster profiles around our 99/70 rules and Bio3 baseline.
- Reduced the regular population to **196 monsters**, with **15-second respawns**. Regulars are level 96-98; elites and MVPs are level 99.
- Added classic class-based skill profiles and corrected MVP reward classification.
- Restored the Bio3 entrance at `lhz_dun03 239,78` and the return portal at `lhz_dun04 244,61`.
- Removed Renewal drops, including Bio4 cards, Bradium and Carnium; retained classic-compatible items and reduced box/album chances for our rates. No direct Bio4 cards means no Mercy card guarantee here.
- Kept Warp Portal, Ice Wall and Loki's Veil blocked even during MVP respawn downtime.

The dungeon guide lists all monster tiers, base EXP, retained rewards and respawn rules. This is the initial party-endgame balance profile; difficulty can be refined using party feedback.

## Main Office

The [Main Office guide](../main-office.md) now brings together current directions, a numbered service map and all eleven office NPCs.

- Enter at **Prontera 164,167**, beneath the **Main Office** bubble; arrive at **main_office 127,38**.
- Leave through the **5x5 return portal centered on 127,41**, which returns to **Prontera 162,167**. The outside entrance uses a **3x3** trigger.
- Find Job Master, Kafra Services, Platinum Skills, Build Manager, Reset Girl, Gunslinger Supplies, Putty, Jackie, Enhanced Tool Dealer, Plagiarism Tutor and Salvia inside.
- Service-name bubbles identify each NPC. Teleport and return items are disabled inside; use the doorway to leave.

The [Character Services guide](../character-services.md) now documents Job Master, Stylist and Healer:
