## Independent baby characters

- Added **Sister Elara**, temporarily at **Prontera 136,196**: `/navi prontera 136/196`.
- Eligible regular Novices and first classes, including Ninja, Taekwon, Gunslinger and Super Novice, can convert for **5,000,000z** without adoption.
- Conversion preserves Base/Job Levels, EXP, skills and allocated stats. Normal baby equipment restrictions apply; the maximum base stat remains **99**.
- Second and later classes, transcended characters, existing babies and adopted/pending-adoption characters cannot use the service.
- Baby first classes can advance while remaining babies. The Star Gladiator and Soul Linker quests now recognize Baby Taekwon.
- Babies cannot transcend or become adults through job changes.
- **Baby Novice is permanently unable to change class**, including to Super Baby. The NPC requires an additional confirmation before taking payment.
- Cancelling is free; a successful conversion charges the fee once. The conversion persists after relogging.
