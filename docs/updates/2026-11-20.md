# November 20, 2026

## Astrid's headgear quests

**Astrid** is available at **Prontera 147,187**: `/navi prontera 147/187`.

- Craft **22 selected headgears** using Vestige Coins and the listed materials.
- Browse headgear pictures in her shop, select one and click **Buy** to open its requirements.
- See **owned/required** counts, with missing materials in **red** and complete requirements in **green**.
- Choose **Purchase**, try on the headgear with **Preview**, or **Cancel**. Previewing preserves equipment and bonuses and restores your normal appearance afterward.
- Craft one headgear per purchase, with **no Zeny fee**. Equipped, refined, carded, favorite and other protected materials are not consumed.
- Headgear names, descriptions and effects follow the configured quest catalogue. **Crimson Rose** has a **fixed 1% chance** to drop a Witherless Rose on a monster kill.

The [Headgear Quests guide](../headgear-quests.md) includes Astrid's picture and a three-column catalogue with headgear icons, illustrated ingredients, exact quantities and descriptions.

## Visual AFK Status

After **60 seconds idle** on an eligible hunting map, an **AFK sign** replaces the upper-headgear appearance. Movement, attacks, skill use and item use clear it immediately. Equipment and its bonuses remain unchanged.

Towns and their interiors, autotrade, open vending/buying shops and WoE castle maps are excluded. See [Visual AFK Status](../visual-afk.md).
