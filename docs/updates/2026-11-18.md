# November 18, 2026

## Novice Training Grounds

Every newly created character now starts in the [Novice Training Grounds](../novice-training.md). Existing characters keep their current locations and progress.

- Guided lessons cover controls, equipment, items, skills and combat.
- Defeat five Porings, then complete at least one of six first-class practice lessons. These lessons do not lock the final class choice.
- Graduate at **Job Level 10** with a starter package of supplies, six equipment pieces and **10,000z**, available once per character.
- The **10,000z replaces Kafra Transportation tickets**. Storage tickets remain part of the package.
- Graduation sends you to **Main Office 127,38** and saves your position there. Spend Basic Skill points, then visit Job Master at **122,38**.
- Training progress survives relogging. Combat Brade provides free recovery; training monsters give EXP and quest progress without loot, cards or Vestige Coins.

The dedicated guide contains the full reward catalogue, NPC locations, class-lesson hunts and graduation requirements. It is listed under **Getting Started**.

## Healer options

The [Healer at Prontera 152,187](../character-services.md#healer) offers two paid recovery choices:

- **1,000z:** full HP and SP.
- **10,000z:** full HP and SP, plus **Level 10 Blessing and Increase AGI for ten minutes**.

The Character Services guide now reflects these options. Free tutorial recovery remains available through combat Brade while training.

## Warper

The [Warper at Prontera 159,187](../warper.md) offers three travel categories:

| Category | Fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Nearby Midgard towns | **2,000z** |
| Distant Midgard towns | **3,600z** |
| New World | **25,000z** |
| Islands | **20,000z** |

- **Islands** combines the former Foreign Lands categories into one menu.
- Destination lists show the price. Selecting a destination charges its fee and **warps immediately**, without a separate confirmation.
- New World towns remain hidden until that character completes **Onward to the New World**. El Dicastes also requires visitor registration, and Eclage requires immigration completion.
- **Access requirements** explains outstanding quests. Previously completed quests are recognized automatically.
- **Jawaii** is available for **3,600z**, without marriage or quest requirements through the Warper.
- Cancellation, insufficient Zeny and unavailable destinations do not charge a fee.

The dedicated guide lists all 34 destinations and their access rules under **Getting Started**.

## Pet System and evolution

The [Pet System](../pet-system.md) guide and [illustrated catalogue](../pet-catalogue.md) now cover the active companion update:

- **115 pet entries** with pet pictures, food and accessory icons, auto-feed availability and owner bonuses by intimacy.
- **43 evolution paths**, with pictures and exact quantities for every ingredient.
- Hunger decreases by **3 every 60 seconds**; eligible pets support auto-feed. Owner death costs **5 intimacy**, and starvation returns the pet to its egg at **1 intimacy**.
- Incubator cancellation preserves the active pet and item. Hatching and pet switching now complete correctly.
- Evolution requires **Loyal intimacy (900+)**, preserves a custom nickname and replaces the original egg with the evolved egg. New forms start at **Neutral (250)** with full hunger.
- Listed rare egg drops use fixed final chances, unaffected by server rates or Bubble Gum.
- Recipes use Drops Card, Vestige Coins and Skel-Bones for the approved classic-compatible substitutions.
- Incubus keeps its existing server bonus. Blue Moon Loli Ruri's Kyrie effect uses a **5% physical-hit trigger**.
- Domovoi is excluded. Pet Stylist and unimplemented seasonal/Old Glast Heim acquisition sources are not presented as active services.
