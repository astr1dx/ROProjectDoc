# November 16, 2026

## Adjusted NPC sell prices

Selected items now pay less Zeny when sold to NPCs. The update covers **82 table entries across 86 item IDs**, including ordinary equipment variants.

The aim is to reduce Zeny creation from high-volume farming, encourage a wider choice of hunting activities and reduce the return from automated farming. These changes support a healthier trading economy; they do not replace other measures against abuse.

- **Crystal Blue (#991): 500z to 250z**, our server-specific choice.
- All listed prices are base resale values; Merchant Overcharge still applies normally.
- NPC purchase prices, drop rates, item effects and restrictions retain their existing behavior.
- Same-name quest, rental and alternate consumable items are excluded unless explicitly listed by ID.

The [Adjusted NPC sell prices guide](../adjusted-prices.md) contains the full previous/current table and explains the balancing goals. Supply purchase prices remain in [Convenient Dealers](../dealers.md).

See the [illustrated Card Exchange catalogue](../card-exchange.md#current-reward-list) for exact item IDs, food stats and reward variants.

## Vestige Coin System

- Added **Vestige Coin (#35363)**, a zero-weight, unbound and tradable currency.
- Eligible monster deaths have a fixed **5% chance** to drop one coin. Familiar, Female/Male Thief Bug and Orc Zombie use **1%**; listed eggs, plants and low-risk species are excluded.
- Server rates, Bubble Gum and other modifiers do not increase coin chances.
- Existing quests and events have not been changed to grant coins.

See [Vestige Coin System](../vestige-coins.md) for the full exception list, loot rules and Autoloot setup.

## Shared Bubble Gum adjustment

**Bubble Gum (#12210)** now grants **+150% item drops for 30 minutes**, replacing +200%. Lydia and Putty offer the same item: **1,000 Vestige Coins** or **55 card points**, respectively. Existing copies use the revised effect when consumed. Vestige Coin drops remain fixed.

## Area Loot

- Added **`@arealoot`** and **`@settings` > Area Loot (3x3)**.
- A successful clicked pickup also tries eligible ground items in the **3x3 square centered on that item**, including diagonals.
- The setting is **Off by default** and saved **per character**.
- Normal ownership, party sharing, weight and inventory limits remain in effect. The pickup square does not chain to other areas.
- Area Loot is manual pickup and is independent of Autoloot ignore lists.

See [Area Loot](../area-loot.md).

## Mercy System

- Added an automatic guarantee after **5,000 eligible kills of the same normal monster without its card dropping**.
- Progress persists **per character and monster ID**; existing recorded cardless streaks count toward the threshold.
- Natural or guaranteed card drops reset the streak, even when the card remains on the ground or goes to a party member.
- Natural drops take priority, preventing an extra guaranteed copy on the same kill.
- **MVPs, mini-bosses and ineligible summons are excluded.**
- Mercy progress appears in **`@kc` / `@killcount`**. Resetting the visible tracker does not reset Mercy.

See [Mercy System](../mercy-system.md) for credit, exclusions and delivery rules.

## Refining System

- All standard equipment refiners and Suhnbi offer **Classic** or **Visual** refining.
- **Classic:** normal ores only, equipped gear, and single/safe-limit/target-level refining. Bulk orders charge only attempted upgrades and stop on failure or insufficient resources.
- **Visual:** enriched ores only, inventory selection, shown success chances and one animated attempt at a time. Each attempt costs one ore and **2,000z**.
- Both interfaces keep **Pre-Renewal rules** and the **+10 cap**. Failure destroys the equipment. **HD ores are not offered.**
- Standardized enriched ores on **Oridecon #7620** and **Elunium #7619**. Existing duplicate ores were converted with quantities preserved, leaving one catalyst option per equipment type.

See [Refining System](../refining.md) for both success-rate tables, fees and NPC locations.
