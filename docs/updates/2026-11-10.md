# November 10, 2026

## Personal settings and channels

- `@settings` saves display/chat preferences for the account and restores them on login. Configure these preferences through the menu.
- Song muting silences subsequent Bard/Dancer song audio while keeping musical notes and ground effects. Turning muting Off restores the audio.
- Existing and new players automatically join **#main**, **#recruit** and **#market**. Saved channel Off choices remain respected.
- Storage withdrawal limits and farming/storage lists are saved **per character**.

[Personal settings guide](../personal-settings.md)

## Advanced autoloot

- `@lootconfig` and **@settings > Autoloot** open the same saved configuration.
- Main Autoloot uses the **original database drop chance**, with a threshold from **0-100%** and up to two decimal places.
- Ignore up to **100 item IDs** and create **40 groups of 100 items**, with one active group at a time.
- Active-group items override the ignore list and can be collected while Main Autoloot is Off.
- Main Autoloot at 100% with Jellopy **#909** ignored leaves it on the ground, provided it is not in the active group. Manual pickup remains available.
- Fixed the disconnect/server-unavailable issue associated with ignored ground drops and pickup.

[Advanced autoloot guide](../autoloot.md)

## Restock and Quick Store

- Added **40 groups of 40 items per type**, saved per character. One Restock and one Quick Store group can be active independently.
- Configure through **@settings > Restock Supplies / Quick Store**, or `@restockconfig` / `@qstoreconfig`.
- `@restock` refills inventory up to saved target totals, from **1-30,000** per item. It remains **strictly below 90% weight** and respects a lower personal withdrawal limit.
- `@qstore` deposits **all eligible inventory units** of the listed item IDs. **No quantity or Keep value is configured.** Add Jellopy **#909** to deposit all eligible Jellopy.
- Existing Quick Store lists keep their names, item IDs and active choices; previous Keep amounts are discarded.
- Transfers require **Main Storage already open in a town**, including Midgard Expedition Camp, Manuk and Splendide. Consumables, stackable miscellaneous items, loose cards and loose ammunition are supported. Normal capacity and item protections remain.

[Fast storage guide](../fast-storage.md)

## Kill Count

- `@kc` / `@killcount` detect the last credited monster when no tracking target is active. **Start confirmation is required**; opening or cancelling the menu changes nothing.
- Only **one monster type** is actively tracked, with no five-slot list. While active, `@kc` opens that monster's summary; an explicit monster ID can propose a replacement.
- Private overhead messages show **`Hill Wind kills: 6,409`**, without a character-name prefix.
- Summaries show **Total kills**, **Kills without card**, **Cards dropped**, and the active **Tracked kills** count.
- Stop and Reset affect the overhead counter, preserving recorded history. The selected monster persists per character; its tracked count restarts on login.
- Card statistics count actual generated drops, including cards left on the ground. Companion kills credit their owner.

[Kill Count guide](../kill-count.md)

## Card Drop Announcements

- Shortened the message to **`Tubbs has obtained their 1st [Lude Card] on the 559th kill.`**
- Credit the successful recipient after normal pickup, autoloot or pet delivery. Pet-collected cards credit the owner.
- Party-shared cards and cards collected from somebody else's kill show the received-copy ordinal without a kill count.
- Each receipt creates a single **Public-filtered** event, respecting **@settings > Announcement Config > Drop**.
- Received-copy counters, Kill Count's generated-card statistics and personal card artwork remain separate.

## Kill Steal Protection

- **`@noks`** and **@settings > Kill Steal Protection** open the same saved mode menu. Default **Party**, saved per character.
- **Self** allows only the owner and their companions; **Party** or **Guild** allows the matching group. Protection can also be disabled.
- Locks begin on eligible monster targeting or positive damage from outside aggro range. There is no protected-monster count limit.
- Release after **5 seconds of owner inactivity**, **15 seconds from the first unauthorized attempt**, or **30 seconds from the initial lock**. Attacks, skills and movement count as activity; chat does not.
- Repeated attacks do not refresh the contest deadline or hard cap, and a released encounter does not immediately re-lock.
- **`@nokscheck`** privately lists party modes and marks unavailable/offline members unchecked.
- MVPs, Boss-mode mini-bosses, native slaves, towns and AllowKS/PvP/GvG/Battleground maps are excluded.
- **Confirmed in-game:** Self blocks outsiders; Party allows party members. The integrated suite passed **3,056 checks**, including **220 protection checks**.
