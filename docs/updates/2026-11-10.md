# November 10, 2026

## Personal settings and channels

- `@settings` saves display/chat preferences for the account and restores them on login. Configure these preferences through the menu.
- Song muting silences subsequent Bard/Dancer song audio while keeping musical notes and ground effects. Turning muting Off restores the audio.
- Existing and new players automatically join **#main**, **#recruit** and **#market**. Saved channel Off choices remain respected.
- Storage withdrawal limits and farming/storage lists are saved **per character**.

[Personal settings guide](../personal-settings.md)

## Advanced autoloot

- `@lootconfig` and **@settings > Autoloot** open the same saved configuration.
- Main Autoloot uses the **original database drop chance**, with a threshold from **0-100%** and up to two decimal places.
- Ignore up to **100 item IDs** and create **40 groups of 100 items**, with one active group at a time.
- Active-group items override the ignore list and can be collected while Main Autoloot is Off.
- Main Autoloot at 100% with Jellopy **#909** ignored leaves it on the ground, provided it is not in the active group. Manual pickup remains available.
- Fixed the disconnect/server-unavailable issue associated with ignored ground drops and pickup.

[Advanced autoloot guide](../autoloot.md)

## Restock and Quick Store

- Added **40 groups of 40 items per type**, saved per character. One Restock and one Quick Store group can be active independently.
- Configure through **@settings > Restock Supplies / Quick Store**, or `@restockconfig` / `@qstoreconfig`.
- `@restock` refills inventory up to saved target totals, from **1-30,000** per item. It remains **strictly below 90% weight** and respects a lower personal withdrawal limit.
- `@qstore` deposits **all eligible inventory units** of the listed item IDs. **No quantity or Keep value is configured.** Add Jellopy **#909** to deposit all eligible Jellopy.
- Existing Quick Store lists keep their names, item IDs and active choices; previous Keep amounts are discarded.
- Transfers require **Main Storage already open in a town**, including Midgard Expedition Camp, Manuk and Splendide. Consumables, stackable miscellaneous items, loose cards and loose ammunition are supported. Normal capacity and item protections remain.
