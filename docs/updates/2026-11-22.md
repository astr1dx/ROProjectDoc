## Endless Tower

The [Endless Tower](../endless-tower.md) party challenge is available through **Captain Janssen at Prontera 217,187**.

- Start with **six or more party members**, each on a different account and **Base Level 85+**.
- Gather on **Misty Island**. The leader starts at the Tower Protection Stone and everyone confirms **`!ready` in public chat within 60 seconds**.
- Receive **one run per account per week**, shared across characters. Reset is **Monday at 06:00 Philippines time**. Readiness failures do not consume a run.
- Climb the floors within **four hours**, without floor skipping. Only the original starting characters can rejoin the party's current floor while someone remains inside.
- The tower closes when every original member leaves or disconnects. The weekly allowance remains consumed.
- **Personal/Guild Storage is blocked inside; RODEX remains available.** Bubble Gum does not increase tower drops.
- Defeat Naght Sieger and exit through **Lost Soul** to record completion. Individual party-member reports show damage, healing, deaths and monster kills near the exit.
- Lost Soul combines the two Twin Edge of Naght Sieger swords into **Violet Fear**.

The guide includes **monster sprites, IDs and quantities in a table for every floor**, including Floor 99's random groups, Entweihen Crothen's thorn spawns and the final encounter waves.

## Misty Island travel and services

- Speak to the island **Warp Helper** to unlock **Misty Island - 5,000z** in the [Warper](../warper.md#misty-island).
- The island **Kafra Employee** provides Main Storage, Guild Storage and return to Prontera.
- The **Tower Protection Stone** offers run creation, current-floor re-entry and weekly run information. **`@instanceinfo`** also shows the remaining allowance.
