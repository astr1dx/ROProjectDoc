## Endless Tower

The [Endless Tower](../endless-tower.md) party challenge is available through **Captain Janssen at Prontera 217,187**.

- Start with **six or more party members**, each on a different account and **Base Level 85+**.
- Gather on **Misty Island**. The leader starts at the Tower Protection Stone and everyone confirms **`!ready` in public chat within 60 seconds**.
- Receive **one run per account per week**, shared across characters. Reset is **Monday at 06:00 Philippines time**. Readiness failures do not consume a run.
- Climb the floors within **four hours**, without floor skipping. Only the original starting characters can rejoin the party's current floor while someone remains inside.
- The tower closes when every original member leaves or disconnects. The weekly allowance remains consumed.
- **Personal/Guild Storage is blocked inside; RODEX remains available.** Bubble Gum does not increase tower drops.
- Defeat Naght Sieger and exit through **Lost Soul** to record completion. Individual party-member reports show damage, healing, deaths and monster kills near the exit.
- Lost Soul combines the two Twin Edge of Naght Sieger swords into **Violet Fear**.

The guide includes **monster sprites, IDs and quantities in a table for every floor**, including Floor 99's random groups, Entweihen Crothen's thorn spawns and the final encounter waves.

## Endless Cellar

The [Endless Cellar](../endless-cellar.md) is available at the **Cellar Protection Stone, Misty Island 82,110**.

- Descend **77 floors**, following the Tower rooms in reverse and finishing in the final Tower arena.
- Enter with **six or more party members**, all **Base Level 85+**. Everyone confirms **`!ready` in public chat within 60 seconds** before the four-hour run begins.
- Receive **one Cellar run per account per week**, separate from Endless Tower. Both reset **Monday at 06:00 Philippines time**.
- Original starting characters can rejoin the current floor while another original member remains inside. Personal/Guild Storage is blocked; RODEX remains available.
- Wounded Morroc and reduced-drop final encounters use **10% of normal configured drop rates**. The two randomly selected Bio Labs MVPs retain their normal rates.
- The finale progresses through Poring, two Bio Labs MVPs, Super Baphomet with Piamettes/Wish Maidens, and Pori Pori with its final wave.
- After the clear, claim through **Arc Pori Pori** within the five-minute exit window. Each original member receives one **Old Card Album, Bloody Branch, Jewelry Box, Poring Box and Cookie Bag**, together through RODEX.
- Party-member copies at the exit show individual damage, healing, deaths and monster kills.

The guide includes **77 illustrated floor tables** with monster sprites, IDs, quantities and the final-stage selection rules.

- Complete the first voyage with Captain Janssen to unlock **Misty Island - 5,000z** in the [Warper](../warper.md#misty-island).
- The island **Kafra Employee** provides Main Storage, Guild Storage and return to Prontera.
