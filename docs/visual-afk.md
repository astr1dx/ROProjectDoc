# Visual AFK Status

Characters show an **AFK sign above their head after 60 seconds of inactivity** on eligible hunting maps. The sign temporarily replaces the upper-headgear appearance. Your equipped item and its bonuses stay unchanged.

Walking, attacking, using a skill or using an item clears the sign immediately and restores your equipped appearance. Equipping an item also counts as activity.

## Where it appears

The indicator applies outside towns and town interiors. It is suppressed while vending, running a buying shop or using autotrade, and on WoE castle maps.

The feature is automatic. It does not change your [Autoloot](autoloot.md) configuration.
