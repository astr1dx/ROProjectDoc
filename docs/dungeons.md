# Hunting and instances

Vestige Rift uses an **adapted Episode 14.2: Eclage** content profile with Classic Pre-Renewal mechanics. The explicit content allowlist controls available maps, monsters and quests. Eclage's original level-100/120 requirements are adapted to level 90; existing level-99 requirements remain.

## Hunting bonuses

Two eligible **regions or dungeon groups** receive a **20-30% Base and Job kill EXP bonus**, rotating every **48-72 hours**. Use **`@bonusmaps`** to see the active groups, included maps and next rotation in Philippine time.

The bonuses multiply weekday/weekend monster EXP rates. Towns, instances, PvP, castles/WoE, Battlegrounds and maps blocking the relevant EXP are excluded. Read [Rotating EXP regions](quality-of-life.md#rotating-exp-regions) for the full rules.

## Natural MVP maps

A map with a natural MVP spawn remains an MVP map while that MVP is dead. Branch summons alone do not give a map that classification, and ordinary mini-boss maps are not automatically included.

**Warp Portal, Ice Wall and Loki's Veil are blocked on natural MVP maps.** See [Server Information](server-rules.md#important-map-definitions) and [Class changes](class-changes.md) for definitions and skill-specific restrictions.

## New World party instance

The **Onward to the New World** combat test uses a private party battlefield. The leader starts with all party members online on the same staging map; a one-person party is allowed. Defeat **50 Allied Soldiers within 20 minutes**. Only the starting leader receives quest progress and completion credit.

See [the New World test guide](quest-changes.md#onward-to-the-new-world-party-battlefield) for starting, re-entry, progress and retry rules.

## Related travel and recovery rules

- [Sensible teleporting](quality-of-life.md#sensible-teleporting): random Fly Wing and Teleport destinations avoid portal/touch areas.
- [Summon timeout](quality-of-life.md#summon-timeout): eligible player-generated monsters expire after at most one hour, without timeout rewards.
- [Increased sitting recovery](quality-of-life.md#increased-sitting-recovery): HP/SP recovery after seven seconds out of combat.
- [Fast storage](fast-storage.md): transfers require an eligible town with Main Storage already open; they are not remote dungeon storage.
