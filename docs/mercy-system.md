# Mercy System

After **5,000 eligible kills of the same normal monster without its card dropping**, the Mercy System guarantees one card. Progress is automatic: you do not need to enable it or start a tracked counter.

## Progress and resets

- Progress is saved **per character and monster ID**, including across logins.
- Any natural or guaranteed card drop resets that monster's Mercy streak to zero.
- The card counts when it **drops**, even if it stays on the ground or party sharing delivers it to another player.
- Buying, trading or receiving a card as a quest reward does not reset Mercy.
- Existing recorded cardless streaks count toward the guarantee. Missing history starts at zero.

The normal card roll happens first. If the threshold kill naturally drops the card, Mercy does not add a second copy. A character whose recorded streak was already 5,000 or higher receives one guarantee on the next eligible kill, rather than retroactive batches of cards.

## Eligible monsters

Mercy applies only to **normal monsters with an available card in their regular drop table**.

**MVPs and mini-bosses are excluded**, including mini-bosses such as Vocal and Bloody Knight. Player-created summons, minions, clones, guardians and deaths that cannot award ordinary loot do not advance Mercy.

Kills credited to you through qualifying pets, homunculi, mercenaries or elementals follow the normal ownership rules. Different monster IDs have separate progress, even when their names are similar.

## Check your progress

Use **`@kc`** or **`@killcount`** to view **Mercy progress: current / 5,000** in an eligible monster's summary. Ineligible monsters are marked accordingly.

Starting, stopping or resetting the visible [Kill Count](kill-count.md) tracker does not change your Mercy streak.

## Collecting the card

A guaranteed card follows normal loot ownership, party sharing, inventory-capacity and [Autoloot](autoloot.md) rules. If it falls to the ground, collect it normally or with [Area Loot](area-loot.md). A personal message tells the credited killer when Mercy guarantees a card.

Mercy does not permanently raise card drop rates. [Card Drop Announcements](card-drop-announcements.md) still occur when somebody successfully receives the card.
