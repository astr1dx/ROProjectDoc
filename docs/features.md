# Features and enhancements

Explore the systems and adjustments available on Vestige Rift. Guides describe our configured server behavior; individual features retain the restrictions explained on their pages.

## Features

- [Vendor system](vendor-system.md): Market Street, shop searches, pricing rules and offline vending with same-account character switching.
- [Build Manager](build-manager.md): ten stat and ten skill slots per character, free saving and 5,000z loading.
- [Personal settings](personal-settings.md): saved display, sound, notification and channel preferences.
- [Advanced autoloot](autoloot.md): drop-chance filters, ignored items and named farming groups.
- [Fast storage](fast-storage.md): target-based Restock and item-only Quick Store lists.

- [Kill Steal Protection](kill-steal-protection.md): saved Self/Party/Guild modes, fixed release timers and party checks.
- [Kill Count](kill-count.md): confirmed single-monster tracking, private overhead counts and saved monster/card statistics.

- [Homunculus and Pet Commands](companion-commands.md): current/growth stats, companion speech and owner-attributed chat rules.

- [Baby Characters](baby-characters.md): independent conversion, retained progress and baby job-progression rules.

## Changes

- [Class changes](class-changes.md): skill adjustments and custom platinum skills, grouped by class.
- [Quest changes](quest-changes.md): The Sign retries, the instanced New World test and bulk ore downgrading.
- [Quality of life](quality-of-life.md): recovery, mail, party buff indicators, bulk purchases, teleporting and more.
- [Server Information](server-rules.md): rates, caps, the content profile and important map definitions.
