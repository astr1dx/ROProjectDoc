# Kill Count

Updated: **November 10, 2026**.

Use **`@kc`** or **`@killcount`** to review a monster's kill history and choose whether to show a private counter above your character. Only **one monster type** can be actively tracked at a time. Your recorded statistics continue to update even when the overhead counter is Off.

## Start tracking your last kill

1. Kill the monster you want to track.
2. Type **`@kc`**. When nothing is being tracked, the menu detects the most recent monster credited to you during this login.
3. Review its summary, then choose **Start tracking <monster> kills**.
4. Kill more of that monster to see your tracked count above your character.

Opening the menu or pressing Cancel does **not** start tracking. The confirmation appears privately above your character:

> You started tracking Hill Wind kills.

Each subsequent credited kill shows:

> Hill Wind kills: 6,409

The message has **no character-name prefix** and is visible only to you. There is no five-monster list or capacity indicator.

## Read the summary

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Total kills | Your character's recorded credited kills of this monster. |
| Kills without card | Credited kills since this monster most recently generated a card. The card-generating kill resets this streak to zero. |
| Cards dropped | Cards generated by this monster on kills credited to you, including cards left on the ground or shared with another player. |
| Tracked kills | Kills recorded by the active overhead counter since you started or reset it during this login. Shown while tracking is active. |

The numbers are grouped with commas. Card pickup, autoloot and pet delivery do not count a generated card a second time. Trades, storage withdrawals and NPC rewards do not add monster-card drops.

Existing total-kill records are retained. Older per-monster card-drop history was not recorded, so **Cards dropped** and **Kills without card** begin from the available history under this system; missing historical drops are not reconstructed.

## Stop, reset or change the monster

Run **`@kc`** again while tracking to open the active monster's summary, even if you have since killed something else.

| Choice | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Stop tracking <monster> kills | Turn off the overhead counter and clear its tracked count. |
| Reset track kills for <monster> | Reset only the tracked count to zero and keep tracking. |
| Cancel | Keep the current selection and count. |

Neither Stop nor Reset changes **Total kills**, **Cards dropped** or **Kills without card**.

To propose a different monster, use **`@kc <monster ID>`**, for example **`@kc 1002`** for Poring. Review the summary and choose Start to replace the active monster. Cancelling keeps your previous selection. `@killcount <monster ID>` works the same way.

## Persistence and kill credit

- The confirmed monster selection and recorded statistics are saved **per character**.
- The tracked overhead count starts at zero and restarts when you log in again. Earlier kills are not added retroactively.
- Death and map changes do not reset the tracked count.
- Kills by your **pet, homunculus, mercenary or elemental** credit you as their owner.
- Being in the party alone does not grant a credited kill. No-reward scripted removals and summon timeouts do not count as hunting kills.

## Card drops versus card receipts

Kill Count records a card when the monster **generates it**. [Card Drop Announcements](card-drop-announcements.md) instead credit the character who **successfully receives it**. These can be different players after party sharing or a manual pickup.

For example, a card left on the ground increases the killing owner's Cards dropped total, but it does not produce a receipt announcement until somebody collects it.
