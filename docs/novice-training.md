# Novice Training Grounds

Updated: **November 18, 2026**.

Every newly created character starts as a Novice in the training grounds. Learn the controls, practice combat and explore the first classes before graduating with **Job Level 10**, starter equipment, supplies and **10,000z**.

!!! info "Starting your adventure"
    New characters arrive at **new_zone01 53,111**, beside Sprakki. Existing characters keep their current locations and progress. Graduation rewards are available **once per character**.

## Your training route

1. **Meet Sprakki** at **new_zone01 53,114**. Learn movement, camera controls and useful shortcuts, then cross the bridge and enter the building at **148,112**.
2. **Learn equipment and items with Brade** at **new_zone02 100,105**. Keep your starting Knife and Cotton Shirt equipped and answer the lesson question. Brade gives you **10 Novice Potions** for practice.
3. **Learn skills with Jinha** at **new_zone02 115,120**. Answer his skill-point question and learn **First Aid**, then enter the hunting grounds through **73,106**.
4. **Defeat five Porings**, then report to Brade at **new_zone03 96,30**. Use **Alt+U** to follow your quest progress.
5. **Complete at least one class lesson** with a guide listed below. Each lesson includes a short three-monster hunt. Return to the same guide to finish it.
6. **Ask combat Brade to proceed to graduation**, then speak to **Hanson** at **new_zone04 100,24**. Confirm graduation to receive your package and enter [Main Office](main-office.md).

Training progress is saved when you log out. Combat Brade offers free HP/SP recovery while you are training. If you die, you return to your current training checkpoint.

### Zeny and levels

- **10,000z**, replacing Kafra Transportation tickets. Transportation tickets are not included.
- **Job Level 10**, ready for your first job change.
- Base levels follow the server's existing EXP rates; graduation does not set a fixed Base Level.

Hanson checks that you have enough inventory space, carrying capacity and room below the Zeny cap for the full package. If there is not enough room, you can return after making space; the rewards remain unclaimed.

## After graduation

You arrive at **Main Office 127,38**, which also becomes your save point.

- Spend your Novice skill points on **Basic Skill** before requesting a job change.
- Visit **Job Master at 122,38** to choose an eligible class. See [Character Services](character-services.md#job-master) for requirements.
- Find **Kafra Services at 131,38** for saving and storage.
- Use the [Main Office service map](main-office.md) to find platinum skills, supplies and other services.
- Open **`@settings`** to configure your preferences, then follow [How to Start](getting-started.md) for hunting tools and rewards.

## Training-ground rules

The grounds are for learning, with EXP and quest kill progress enabled. Monsters here do **not** drop normal loot, cards or Vestige Coins. Teleportation, return items and monster-summoning branches are disabled; follow the tutorial's doors and NPCs to advance.

Once a character graduates, the training grounds cannot be used to claim another starter package.
