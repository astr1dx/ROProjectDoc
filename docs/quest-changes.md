# Quest changes

Updated: **November 7, 2026**.

These quests keep their existing access requirements and story progression, with the changes below.

| Quest | Original behavior | Ad Infinitum changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Sign** | Failing Part 6A/6B grants EXP and permanently blocks progress until a GM resets it. | Failure starts a **15-minute cooldown**. Gen returns the **Symbol of the Nine Realms** so you can retry. Failed attempts no longer grant EXP. |
| **Onward to the New World** | A shared battlefield requires 40 Allied Soldier kills within 15 minutes. | Each party gets a **private battlefield** with **50 kills in 20 minutes**. Only the starting leader receives quest credit. The quest log tracks progress, and soldiers respawn immediately. |
| **Ore downgrading** | The Utan Shaman divides up to ten pure elemental stones per visit. | Choose **one stone** or the **entire carried stack** of the selected stone type. |

## The Sign: retrying a failed ordeal

Failing Part 6A or 6B, including running out of time in the challenge room, starts a **15-minute cooldown**. The cooldown continues through logout and server restarts.

1. Speak to **Gen**, the Depressing Man in Niflheim: `/navi niflheim 146/241`.
2. Wait until the cooldown has finished. Gen tells you how much time remains if you return early.
3. Make room for the **Symbol of the Nine Realms**. Gen returns it without creating an extra copy if you already carry one.
4. Bring the symbol to **Kirkena or Serin** to restart from the symbol hand-in.

Leftover loan items from the failed attempt are cleared when Gen resets that part of the quest. Successful quest rewards remain unchanged; **failed attempts award no EXP**.

!!! note "Previously failed characters"
    If your character was already stuck in an old failed state, speaking to Gen starts its first 15-minute retry cooldown.

## Onward to the New World: party battlefield

Speak to **Munkenro** when you reach his combat test: `/navi moc_fild22b 230/197`.

### Starting the test

- You must be in a party. A **one-person party is allowed**.
- **Only the party leader** can start the test.
- **Every party member must be online and on the same public staging map** when the leader starts.
- Your party enters its own battlefield, allowing other parties to run their tests independently.

### Objective and credit

Defeat **50 Allied Soldiers within 20 minutes**. Defeated soldiers respawn immediately while the test is active.

The **starting leader's quest log** shows the kill objective and progress. Party members can help defeat the soldiers, but **only the starting leader advances this quest and receives its completion reward**. Eligible party kills count across the battlefield while that leader is online, in the instance, and still in the owning party.

### Leaving, finishing, or retrying

Speak to Munkenro on the public staging map to **re-enter an active test** after leaving or reconnecting. Re-entry keeps the original deadline and starting leader; it does not reset the timer.

On success, the party returns to the public staging map and the starting leader speaks to Munkenro to claim the normal quest reward. When time runs out, the party returns without completion credit and the leader can start another attempt. A new attempt resets the kill objective.

## Ore downgrading: one stone or a full stack

Visit the **Utan Shaman** in Umbala: `/navi um_in 44/71`. The existing language and shaman-access quest requirements still apply.

| Pure stone | ID | Rough stone received |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Great Nature | 997 | Green Live |
| Mystic Frozen | 995 | Crystal Blue |
| Flame Heart | 994 | Red Blood |
| Rough Wind | 996 | Wind of Verdure |

Choose the stone type, select **one stone** or your **entire carried stack**, and confirm the exchange. The full-stack option uses only that selected stone type from your inventory.

Each pure stone independently produces **6?10 rough stones**, retaining the original yield. If the result exceeds your carrying weight, inventory space, or item-stack capacity, your original stones are returned and the exchange does not complete. You can also cancel before confirming.
