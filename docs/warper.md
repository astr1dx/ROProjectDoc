# Warper

Updated: **November 22, 2026**.

Travel to **34 towns and Misty Island** through the Warper. Find her at **Prontera 159,187**, marked with a **Warper** bubble:

`/navi prontera 159/187`

## How to travel

1. Speak to the Warper and choose **Midgard Towns**, **New World**, **Islands** or **Misty Island**.
2. Read the fee beside the destination name.
3. Select the town to **pay and warp immediately**. There is no separate Pay and Travel confirmation.

Choose **Back** or **Cancel** to leave without paying. The NPC checks access, destination availability and your Zeny before charging. Insufficient Zeny or an unavailable destination does not charge you; a failed transfer to the destination map returns the travel fee.

## Travel fees

| Category | Fee per trip |
| --- | ---: |
| Nearby Midgard towns | **2,000z** |
| Distant Midgard towns | **3,600z** |
| New World | **25,000z** |
| Islands | **20,000z** |
| Misty Island | **5,000z**, after unlocking the island Warp Helper |

Midgard's nearby/distant groups are based on proximity to Prontera. Each destination has a fixed fee.

## Midgard towns

### Nearby towns - 2,000z

Prontera, Izlude, Geffen, Payon, Alberta, Morroc.

### Distant towns - 3,600z

Aldebaran, Comodo, Umbala, Niflheim, Lutie, Jawaii, Yuno, Hugel, Einbroch, Einbech, Lighthalzen, Rachel, Veins.

**Jawaii is available through this Warper without marriage or quest requirements.** Its travel fee is **3,600z**.

## New World

Every destination below costs **25,000z** and requires completion of **Onward to the New World**.

| Destination | Additional access requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Midgard Expedition Camp | None beyond New World access |
| Splendide | None beyond New World access |
| Manuk | None beyond New World access |
| Mora | None beyond New World access |
| El Dicastes | Complete El Dicastes visitor registration with Clerk Rhawyne |
| Eclage | Complete Eclage immigration with the Security Guard |

### Unlocking destinations

Access belongs to **each character**. Quest-locked towns are hidden from the travel list until that character completes the required entrance quest. They appear automatically the next time you open the menu, including for characters who completed the quest previously.

Choose **Access requirements** in the main menu to see what is still missing. Visiting the map or holding a quest item does not substitute for finishing the quest. Access is checked again before the travel fee is deducted.

For El Dicastes, finish the visitor registration rather than stopping at the invitation. For Eclage, finish the immigration process with the Security Guard. Both also require New World access.

## Islands

The former Foreign Lands categories are combined into **Islands**. All nine destinations cost **20,000z**:

- **Amatsu**
- **Ayothaya**
- **Gonryun**
- **Louyang**
- **Moscovia**
- **Brasilis**
- **Dewata**
- **Malangdo**
- **Port Malaya**

The towns listed here do not require a separate entrance quest through this Warper. Quests that unlock a town's dungeon still apply to that dungeon.

## Misty Island

The **Misty Island - 5,000z** category takes you to the [Endless Tower staging area](endless-tower.md#misty-island-services). First reach the island through **Captain Janssen at Prontera 217,187**, then speak to **Warp Helper at e_tower 75,111**. This unlock is saved for that character.

The destination costs **5,000z** per trip and appears after the unlock. The tower's level, party and weekly admission requirements still apply separately.
