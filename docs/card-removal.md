# Card Removal

Updated: **November 17, 2026**.

The **Wise Old Woman**, marked with a **Card Remover** bubble, removes compounded cards from **equipped items**.

```text
/navi prt_in 28/73
```

Find her in Prontera's original refining building. This service is separate from [Putty's Card Exchange](card-exchange.md), which consumes eligible loose cards for points.

## Cost per attempt

| Requirement | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Base fee | **200,000z** |
| Additional fee | **25,000z per compounded card** |
| Star Crumb | **1** |
| Yellow Gemstone | **1** |

An item with two cards costs **250,000z**, one Star Crumb and one Yellow Gemstone per attempt. Make room in your inventory for the equipment and returned cards.

## Removing cards

1. Equip the item and talk to the Card Remover.
2. Choose the equipment slot to examine.
3. Review its card count, fee and required materials.
4. Read the failure warning. Choose **The item** or **The cards** to protect that side from partial failure and confirm the attempt. Choose **I changed my mind** to cancel without paying.

!!! warning "Removal can destroy equipment and cards"
    Protection applies only to **partial failure**. A total failure can destroy **both**. Attempted removals consume the fee and materials even when they fail.

| Outcome | Chance | Result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Success | **90%** | The equipment and its cards are returned separately. |
| Harmless failure | **2%** | The equipment and cards stay intact; the fee and materials are lost. |
| Partial failure | **6%** | The selected protected side survives; the other side is destroyed. |
| Total failure | **2%** | Both the equipment and its cards are destroyed. |
