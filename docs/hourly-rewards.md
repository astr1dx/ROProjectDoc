# Hourly rewards

Updated: **November 9, 2026**.

Earn points while you play, convert them into Gold Coins, and spend the coins on costumes at the Gold Point Manager.

## Earning points

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Reward rate | **10 points per hour online** |
| Progress | Shared across characters on the same account |
| Partial hours | Saved through logout and server restarts |
| Normal AFK time | Counts while the character remains online |
| Offline vending / autotrade | Does not earn points |
| Point balance limit | **300 points** |

Earning pauses at the 300-point balance limit. Spend points to make room and resume earning. Changing characters does not create a separate balance or reset accumulated partial-hour progress.

## Convert points into Gold Coins

Click the **hourly timer in the top-right corner** to automatically convert every complete bundle of **120 points into one Gold Coin (#7929)**. Remaining points and partial-hour progress are kept.

| Points before clicking | Gold Coins received | Points left |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 120 | 1 | 0 |
| 190 | 1 | 70 |
| 240 | 2 | 0 |
| 300 | 2 | 60 |

One coin requires **12 hours** at the normal reward rate. Clicking with fewer than 120 points leaves the balance intact.

The coins go into the inventory of the character that clicks the timer. If that character lacks the required inventory capacity or weight allowance, points are retained; make room and click again.

!!! info "Choose the character that will spend the coins"
    Points are account-wide, but converted Gold Coins stay with the receiving character. Each coin weighs **1**. Coins cannot be dropped, traded, sold to NPCs, mailed, auctioned, placed in a cart, or put in personal or guild storage.

## Spend your coins

Visit the **Gold Point Manager** in Prontera: `/navi prontera 144/200`.

Click the NPC to open the costume exchange shop. Select the costume, add it to the exchange tray, and confirm the purchase using Gold Coins in your inventory. Your character stays in place while the shop is open; close the shop to walk normally again.

Use the timer to convert points and the NPC to shop. There is no separate point-exchange dialogue to work through.
