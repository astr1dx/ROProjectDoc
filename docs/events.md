# Rewards and activities

Use this section to find the rewards and recurring activities currently documented for Ad Infinitum. Server schedules use **Philippine time (UTC+08:00)**.

## Account rewards

| Activity | How to participate | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily attendance | Claim through Attendance Check, then collect the mailed attachment. One claim per account per day; missed days do not reset progress. | [Attendance rewards](attendance.md) |
| Hourly rewards | Earn online points, click the top-right timer to convert complete 120-point bundles, and spend Gold Coins at the costume shop. | [Hourly rewards](hourly-rewards.md) |
| Card exchange | Exchange eligible loose cards with Putty for account-wide card points. Bloody Branch costs 120 points. | [Card exchange](card-exchange.md) |

Hourly points, Gold Coins and card-exchange points are separate currencies. Check their guides for limits and restrictions.

## Weekend and rotating hunting bonuses

Weekends run **Saturday 00:00 through Monday 00:00**. Monster Base/Job EXP and normal non-card drops rise from **20x to 25x**; static card and boss rates do not change. See [Rates](server-rules.md#rates).

Two hunting regions or dungeon groups also rotate every **48-72 hours**, with **20-30% kill EXP bonuses** that stack with active rates. Use `@bonusmaps` to inspect the current selection; see [Rotating EXP regions](quality-of-life.md#rotating-exp-regions).

## Siege-related settings

RODEX is blocked only inside castles during their relevant active WoE session. It remains available on ordinary maps during WoE. The personal **Hide Headgear and Costumes during WoE** option also applies on active castle maps; configure it through `@settings`.

See [Mail rules](quality-of-life.md#rodex-mail), [Personal settings](personal-settings.md) and [Class changes](class-changes.md) for the restrictions relevant to siege play. These rules do not establish a public WoE schedule.
