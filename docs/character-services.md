# Character Services

Updated: **November 17, 2026**.

Find job changes, appearance options and healing at the NPCs below. These locations match the current server scripts.

## Job Master

The Job Master is inside **[Main Office](main-office.md) at 122,38**, just west of the **127,38** landing. Enter Main Office from **Prontera 164,167** and look for the **Job Master** bubble.

Job changes through this NPC are free. Available choices depend on your current class and progression.

| Change | Current requirement |
| --- | --- |
| First job | Job Level **10** |
| Second job | Job Level **40** or higher, with an eligible first class |
| Super Novice | Base Level **45** and the applicable Novice job requirement |
| Rebirth | An eligible class at Base Level **99**, Job Level **50**, and carried weight **500 or less** |

Spend unused skill points and remove your cart, falcon or mount before proceeding. Transcendent progression follows the previous class path. Eligible platinum skills are granted automatically; the separate [Platinum Skill NPC](main-office.md#service-directory) handles its own class and custom unlocks.

Third and advanced expanded classes are disabled. Baby Novices cannot change class, and baby characters cannot transcend. Use [Sister Elara](baby-characters.md) for independent baby conversion.

## Stylist

The Stylist is at **prt_in 43,114**, inside the **[Coin Exchange building](main-office.md#coin-exchange-building)**. Enter from **Prontera 133,183**, beneath the **Coin Exchange** bubble, and look for **Stylist** inside.

All appearance changes are free. Choose **Hair style**, **Hair color** or **Clothing color**. The dialog shows the current ID and available range.

- **Next / Previous** cycles through the available choices.
- **Choose an ID** lets you enter a specific valid option.
- **Restore original** returns to the look you had when you opened that category.
- **Keep this look** closes the preview with your selected appearance.

Previews apply immediately while browsing. If you want to undo a preview, choose **Restore original** before closing.

### Available styles

Human characters have **42 hairstyles (IDs 1-42)** and **nine hair colors (IDs 0-8)**, including the default hair color. Clothing colors depend on the character's class:

| Class group | Clothing color IDs | Choices |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Novice, High Novice and Baby Novice | **0-8** | 9 |
| Super Novice/Super Baby, Taekwon, Star Gladiator, Soul Linker, Ninja, Gunslinger and corresponding baby classes | **0-7** | 8 |
| First classes, Knight, Priest, Wizard, Blacksmith, Hunter, Assassin, Alchemist, Bard, Dancer and corresponding baby classes | **0-4** | 5 |
| Crusader, Monk, Sage, Rogue and corresponding baby classes; transcendent second classes | **0-3** | 4 |

The menu limits clothing colors to those available for your class. A color supported by your new class is retained through job changes and relogging; an unsupported clothing color resets to the default.

## Healer

Find the **Healer** at **Prontera 152,187**, marked with a **Healer** bubble.

Click to restore **100% HP and SP**, for free. There is **no configured healing cooldown**. This is a recovery service; it does not grant Blessing or Increase AGI.

Healing does not require your character to walk up against the NPC. After healing, you can continue moving normally.
