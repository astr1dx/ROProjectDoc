    🍬 Convenient dealers¶

    Updated: November 9, 2026.

    Find everyday supplies at the existing Tool and Pet dealers throughout the world, with dedicated Ninja and Gunslinger dealers in Prontera.

    Find a dealer¶

    Dealer Where to find it Stock
    Enhanced Tool Dealer 43 existing Tool Dealer locations, including the eight inn dealers 35 everyday supplies and consumables
    Pet Dealer 8 existing Pet Groomer locations 30 pet supplies
    Jackie /navi prontera 132/196 Shuriken, kunai and ninjutsu stones
    Gunslinger Supplies /navi prontera 140/200 Bullets, spheres and the existing sphere-crafting materials

    Enhanced Tool and Pet dealers replace the previous stock at the original NPC locations. There are no separate Tool or Pet dealer additions in Prontera's service row. In Prontera, the existing Tool Dealer is at /navi prt_in 126/76, and the Pet Dealer is at /navi prontera 218/211.

    All prices below are base Zeny prices before applicable discounts. Merchant Discount and Rogue Compulsion Discount apply normally. Eligible exactly-30,000-unit orders use the bulk-purchase and Main Storage rules.

    Custom kunai¶

    These Ninja-only ammunition items are usable from Level 1, cost 10z each before discounts, and have a base NPC resale value of 5z.

    Kunai Item ID Element ATK Weight
    Hamaya Kunai 35360 Holy 30 0.5
    Shadow Kunai 35361 Shadow 30 0.5
    Formless Kunai 35362 Ghost 30 0.5

    Their effects are elemental ammunition; they do not add separate status-infliction procs.

    Ammunition and pet-supply exceptions¶

    The expanded specialist stock includes selected supplies beyond the base Episode 14.2 item profile, adapted for the server's Classic Pre-Renewal rules.

    Bullet Item ID Element ATK Weight Usable from level
    Stone Bullet 13219 Earth 40 0.2 1
    Flare Bullet 13228 Fire 50 0.5 1
    Lightning Bullet 13229 Wind 50 0.5 1
    Ice Bullet 13230 Water 50 0.5 1
    Poison Bullet 13231 Poison 50 0.5 1
    Dark Bullet 13232 Shadow 50 0.5 1

    Poison Bullet and Dark Bullet retain a 5% base chance to inflict Poison or Blind respectively under their normal on-hit conditions. Stone Bullet's former Level 100 requirement is reduced to Level 1.

    The additional pet supplies are Delicious Meat (#11616), Suspicious Bottle (#25231), Luxurious Pet Food (#25377) and Cotton Wads (#25233). Their prices are included in the Pet supplies table.
